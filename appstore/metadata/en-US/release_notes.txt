Radical QR 2.1 — paste it the way you wrote it.

• Appointments. Paste "Dinner Saturday 8pm, 350 Fifth Avenue, New York" and get a calendar event: date, time, place and meeting link are already filled in. Add a title, and it is ready to scan into a calendar.

• Wi-Fi from the card on the table. "Wi-Fi: CafeGuest / Password: …" becomes a code that joins the network. Name, password and security in one small form.

• Contacts from a signature. Paste an email signature and get a contact card: name, phone, email, company, job title, website, address.

• Addresses. An address on its own becomes a code that opens it in Maps. Nothing is looked up from your device.

• Asks when unsure. If the text only might be one of these, the app offers instead of deciding — and "Keep as text" always gives you back exactly what you pasted.

Fixed: typing on the start screen no longer loses the field after the first letter.
Fixed: a few Arabic, Japanese and Chinese translations.

As always: no tracking, no accounts — everything stays on your device.
