Radical QR 2.1.1

• Fixed a crash on iOS 26 and later: choosing the roundest modules could close the app while a code was on screen. The shape is unchanged; it is the drawing that was asking for an impossible corner.

• More room for the code. The name and the mark moved into the navigation bar, so a form and its QR code fit on screen together — it matters on a small iPhone and on a long contact card.

• Help rewritten around what 2.1 does: how text written for people becomes an event, a Wi-Fi code, a contact or an address, with examples you can copy. It now lives in the ⋯ menu, next to Settings.
