Elegant QR codes. Auto-detection. Zero tracking. No subscription.

Radical QR generates every code — links, contacts, Wi-Fi, events — entirely on your device. Paste anything, the app recognizes what it is and produces the right QR. Nothing is sent to a server. Nothing is stored without your consent.

Built for the people who use QR codes every day — freelancers, restaurants, associations, entrepreneurs — and who want something beautiful, fast, and respectful of their privacy.


WHY RADICAL QR

• Auto-detect: paste a URL, a phone number, an email, a vCard, an iCalendar event, a Wi-Fi network — the app understands and produces the right QR, with the right encoding.
• Settings you can see: every option draws its own result. Pick the corner you want, the eye you want, the size you want — there is nothing to read and nothing to guess.
• Duplicate a code: point the camera at an existing QR, or pick a photo of one, and make it yours. Nothing is recorded, nothing is sent.
• Clean export: PNG, JPEG, WebP for free; PDF and SVG vector in Pro, up to 4096 px.
• macOS integration: right-click any URL, contact, or event in Safari, Contacts, or Calendar → "Generate QR Code". Instant.
• Drag & drop: drop a text file, a vCard, or an .ics directly into the app.
• 10 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Arabic, Hindi, Chinese.
